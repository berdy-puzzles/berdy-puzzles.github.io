Zeroed Out

Rules
Normal sudoku
Normal sudoku rules apply, except for the zeros below.
Zeros
Place 9 zeros in the grid. Each zero replaces one of the digits 1⁠–⁠9, and each digit is replaced exactly once.
Each 3×3 box contains exactly one zero.
No isolated zeros: Every zero shares a row or column with at least one other zero.
Example: One box will contain the digits 0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9. Its zero must share a row or column with another zero, but it may not share a row, column or box with a 1 (the digit it replaced).
