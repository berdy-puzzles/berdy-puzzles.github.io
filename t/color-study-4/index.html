Paint by No Numbers

(Published on 21. October 2026 by Berdy) — draft, not yet on Logic Masters

I wanted to try setting a color-based Sudoku that wasn't just a numbers Sudoku with a slightly inconvenient interface. My ambition was to make a puzzle that felt more intuitive to reason through with a color palette than with modular arithmetic. I hope I have achieved that in this puzzle; if not, feel free to solve the numbers based version!

Rules
Color sudoku
Shade every cell. Each row, column and 3×3 box contains red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple once each, plus three gray cells.
The purple cell is given.
Color wheel
Red → orange → yellow → green → blue → purple → back to red. Gray is not on the wheel, so it never appears on a yellow or blue line.
Gradient lines (cadmium yellow)
Along a yellow gradient line, each cell is either the same color as the last or it is the next color going around the color wheel.
One line moves through the color wheel in one direction without reversing.
Constant step lines (ultramarine blue)
Every step along the line moves the same number of positions around the wheel, in the same direction. Different lines may have different step sizes.
Mixing arrows
The colors in all the cells of a shaft mix to make the color in its circle. A mix is the set of primaries it contains.
Primaries: red, yellow and blue.
Mixes: orange = red + yellow, green = yellow + blue, purple = blue + red, gray = all three.
Repeats add nothing: red + red = red, red + orange = orange, orange + blue = gray, and anything mixed with gray is gray. Each shaft is mixed on its own.
Warm / cold cells
A reddish-orange glow inside a cell's edge marks a warm cell (red, orange or yellow); a blue-gray glow marks a cold cell (green, blue or purple).
