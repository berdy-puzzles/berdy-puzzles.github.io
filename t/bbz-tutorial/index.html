Black Box Zero: Tutorial

(Eingestellt am 21. Oktober 2026 von Berdy) — local preview, not on Logic Masters

Intro to black box sudoku, find your first atoms!

Play on SudokuPad →
In this puzzle, there are three pairs of matching shooters, corresponding to three beams.
Rules
Sudoku
Normal 6x6 sudoku rules apply: every row, column and 2x3 box contains the digits 1 to 6 once each.
Atoms
Exactly 3 cells hide an atom.
An atom's digit is smaller than every orthogonally adjacent digit.
Beams
Each pair of matching shooters is joined by one beam. It travels straight from cell to cell, and before each step it looks at the three cells in front of it: straight ahead and the two diagonals.
Hit: if there is an atom straight ahead, the beam gets absorbed by the atom.
Deflection: otherwise, an atom diagonally ahead turns the beam 90°, away from the atom.
Reflection: atoms diagonally ahead on both sides send the beam straight back.
Green entropic beams
Digits along a green beam alternate between low (1-3) and high (4-6).
Purple renban beams
Digits along a purple beam are distinct and consecutive, in any order.
How beams travel
No atom: straight through.
Atom straight ahead: absorbed.
Straight ahead beats diagonal: still absorbed.
Atom diagonally ahead: turns away.
Atoms diagonally ahead on both sides: straight back.
Atom at the edge: deflected before it enters.

The beam turns in the cell before the atom's diagonal. Every cell it passes through, in order, counts as a cell along the beam; a cell it turns in counts once.
